Population, GDP per capita, sectoral shares, urbanization, internet use, trade openness, scientific publications, R&D expenditure, researcher intensity, and renewable internal freshwater resources are drawn from the **World Development Indicators**.

Unless an individual dataset or indicator states otherwise, World Bank open data are made available under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)** together with the World Bank's additional terms.
